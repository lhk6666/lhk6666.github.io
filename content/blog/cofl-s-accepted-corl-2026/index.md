Our paper **CoFL-S: Spatially Queryable Sector Flow Fields for Local
Language-Conditioned Navigation** has been accepted to the
[Conference on Robot Learning (CoRL) 2026](https://www.corl.org/)!

CoFL-S is a low-level vision-language-action framework that predicts a
language-conditioned flow field over the robot's local visible sector and
rolls it out into continuous trajectories. It is trained with frame-level
local supervision distilled from VLN-CE episodes.

- Paper page: [CoFL-S](/publications/cofl-s/)
- arXiv: [2607.02222](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02222)
- Video: [YouTube](https://youtu.be/NJpWBFihxbo)

Many thanks to all my co-authors and to my advisor Moju Zhao. See you at CoRL!
